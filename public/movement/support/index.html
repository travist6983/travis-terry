Common questions

How do I unlock the next movement?
Get the one before it running. Finish every step, then wind it with the crown until it starts.

I bought the game on another phone. How do I get it back?
Download it again from the App Store with the same Apple Account. It is yours; you won't be charged twice.

A part won't drop in.
If it is resting on its seat but riding high, it needs turning: circle a finger around it, slowly. If it slides past without dropping, you are turning too fast.

A screw won't stay tight.
Bring every screw on that bridge down until it touches first. Then tighten each one a little, until it feels snug, and let go.

Something broke.
That is part of the bench. A new part comes from the drawer, free. The master tells you what went wrong.

The alarm didn't ring.
The alarm on Caliber 11 rings only while the game is open. It does not send a notification.

How do I change a watch's dial or strap?
Open the watch from the box and choose Rebuild. When it runs again you can case it with a new dial and strap. It stays in your box meanwhile.

How do I start again from nothing?
Settings, then Reset progress. This clears every build and empties the watch box. It cannot be undone.

Will my progress move to a new phone?
Yes, if you restore the new phone from a backup of the old one.
