Privacy policy

Movement: Watchmaker · Last updated 12 November 2026

The short version

Movement: Watchmaker collects no data about you. Nothing you do in the game leaves your phone.

What the game stores

The game saves three kinds of thing, on your device only:

  • your progress in each movement you are building;
  • your watch box: the watches you have finished, and the dial and strap you chose for each;
  • your settings, such as the alarm time, sound and haptics.

This is kept in the app's own storage on your iPhone. It is included in your normal device backup if you have backups switched on, under your own Apple Account. We cannot see it.

What the game does not do

  • It has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • It contains no advertising.
  • It contains no analytics and no third-party code that tracks you.
  • It does not use your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Motion

In movements with an automatic winding rotor, the game reads which way your phone is tilted so that gravity can swing the rotor. This reading is used at that moment, on your device, and is never stored or sent anywhere.

Purchases

The game is bought once, from Apple's App Store. There is nothing else to buy inside it. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details. Apple's privacy policy covers that purchase.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
