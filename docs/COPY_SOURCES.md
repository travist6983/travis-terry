## How to read this

- **`resume L<n>`**: line n of the source resume text (numbered with `cat -n`). The resume text itself is private; the quotes here are exact.
- **brief:HEADLINE_PROOF** and **brief:CORRECTIONS**: blocks in Travis's build brief. HEADLINE_PROOF holds the only numbers allowed as counted moments.
- **brief:HARD_RULE_n**, **brief:PHASE1_A**, **brief:PHASE4**: site-behavior statements from the brief. They describe the site, not Travis, and are marked *verify in Phase 5 QA* because they are only true once the build does what they say.
- **`decisions:<topic>`**: a row in Travis's DECISIONS file.
- **`decisions:Q<n>`**: Travis's written answer to intake question n, quoted from the answers section of his DECISIONS file.
- **Open:** the visible NEEDED FROM TRAVIS placeholder that renders next to the string until Travis supplies the fact. Every one is also in the open placeholders table.
- Hedged numbers stay hedged. Where the resume summary and a bullet disagree, the copy follows HEADLINE_PROOF wording or Travis's answer, keeping the stronger hedge (for example "about six weeks", "from about a week").
- Public placeholder text names only what Travis needs to supply or approve. The private findings behind a placeholder are not repeated here.
- Atlas UP and EOS One systems are described in the past tense. The quotes keep the resume's own tense, because they are exact.
- The email address is obfuscated in this public file. The phone number is not on the site at all.
- The resume (L62) and HEADLINE_PROOF spell the iOS app with a trailing s. That is a known typo; the site uses "Pocket Chronicle" per DECISIONS.
